## About

I work on **quantitative research**, **systematic trading**, and **financial data engineering**, with a growing interest in **market microstructure**.

Most of what I know came from watching promising backtests fail under proper validation. That experience taught me that robust research is built on clean data, reproducible experiments, and healthy skepticism, not optimistic Sharpe ratios.

I enjoy asking questions, working with messy real-world datasets, designing careful experiments, and building research systems whose results can be trusted.

> **Now:** Reading Stoikov, Cont, Gatheral, and Avellaneda while building a research platform for studying fair value estimation in electronic futures markets.

# 💭 Research Philosophy

> **Good models don't fix bad data.**

Whether the problem is finance, environmental science, or machine learning, I like starting with messy datasets, questioning assumptions, and designing experiments that survive honest evaluation.

To me, **reproducibility matters more than novelty, and careful validation matters more than impressive backtests.**
